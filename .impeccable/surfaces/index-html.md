## Scope

Homepage `index.html` (Persuade). The visual system it establishes is shared by every page through `site.css`, `tools/chrome.mjs` and `assets/css/pages/*.css`.

## Audience, job, action

Business owners and IT leads, developers and security teams, curious citizens, funders. Job: understand in seconds what Tehisabiline does and why it differs. Actions: "Tasuta kaardistus" (email, primary) and "Küsi API-võtit" (secondary).

## Constraints

Copy may be tightened but factual claims and SEO-checked statements stay (H1 must name Küberaudit and keeldudeta ja piirangutega küber-AI). No canvas in the hero. Self-hosted fonts and images only. Not dark, no hacker clichés, motion only where it shows something (owner).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A familiar AI landing page that proves its claim in the first viewport: the same security question gets a refusal from a cloud AI and a permission-bound, actionable answer from Tehisabiline, and audit findings land once. It refuses the category default of a static dashboard screenshot beside a headline.

OWN-WORLD: White reading ground; a saturated brand-blue light ribbon (deep blue through bright azure, sky highlights, a touch of indigo) owns the hero's right half as a raster plate; near-black display type with a navy-to-blue continuation; bright blue primary buttons; a translucent white demo window with one soft elevation and 16px corners; small pill priority chips (Kohe red, 7 päeva amber, Plaani blue).

STORY: The visitor sees agents audit with human review and a cyber-AI that answers legitimate security work within written permission, hosted in Estonia; believes it because the demo shows the difference and the limits stay visible; acts through "Tasuta kaardistus" or "Küsi API-võtit".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav: T logo and name left, five links, blue "Tasuta kaardistus" right. Left half: three-line H1, one-line subline, primary and secondary buttons. Right half: the ribbon bleeding to the right edge, carrying the demo window (prompt bar; Pilve-AI and Tehisabiline answers side by side; three findings with chips; "Inimene vaatab iga leiu üle"). Bottom band: three facts with icons.

FORM: Category standard (canon) pinned by the owner ("tavaline landing AI page, kuid natuke huvitavam", reference Stripe); approved comp `.impeccable/mocks/comp-4-combined.png` (merge of comps 2 and 3, bolder blue); seed key f8d3f78e.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The hero demo plays once on load (question, refusal, answer, findings) and settles; reduced motion shows the settled state; a replay control lets the visitor run it again.

## Confirmed revisions

Owner requested less blue after approving the combined comp: the ribbon is lighter with more white at its left and bottom edges. On mobile, the header and headline always remain on white; the ribbon sits behind the demo only. Subpage header ribbons are hidden below 900px.

Measured and implemented fonts: Host Grotesk 800 for display, Hanken Grotesk for text, JetBrains Mono for code. Hardware ordered / Estonian hosting coming / API pilot remain visible in the facts row.

Owner waived the additional plan-and-asset browser approval page: "Panin kinni, vahet pole, jätkame". The comp remains the visual reference; its build-phase tooling was not passed or falsely marked approved. Finish review and runtime verification remain required.
