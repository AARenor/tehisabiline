ISO 27001 INTERNAL AUDIT REPORT: TEMPLATE
==========================================

Plain-text skeleton for an internal audit report under clause 9.2 of
ISO/IEC 27001:2022. Fill every angle-bracket field. Delete a section only if
you say why under "Scope and exclusions" or "Limitations". The report has two
readers: the process owner who must act, and the top manager whose review
(clause 9.3) needs the results.

Published by Tehisabiline OU, https://tehisabiline.ee/en/iso-27001/audit-checklist/
Version 1.0, 2026-11-05. Free to copy and adapt inside your organisation.

Pair it with the audit checklist that items are tested against:
https://tehisabiline.ee/assets/downloads/iso-27001-audit-checklist.csv

Prepared by:             <auditor name, role>

---------------------

                          "all applicable controls for this area">
Criteria:                <ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses and Annex A, internal
                          policy versions and IDs, legal and contractual

                          version>

Independence statement:  <why the auditor is independent of the area audited,
                          for example a peer from another unit, an external
                          auditor engaged for the task, or a rotation record>

                          affects the conclusion>


4. SUMMARY OF RESULTS
---------------------
Controls tested:         <n>
Conforming:              <n>
Partially conforming:    <n>
Nonconforming:           <n>
Not tested:              <n, with the reason>
Overall opinion:         <the ISMS area audited [conforms / conforms with
                          findings / does not conform] with the criteria, in
                          one or two sentences a board member can act on>
Repeat findings:         <n, listing the finding IDs that were already open>


5. FINDINGS
-----------
One block per finding. Do not merge findings to reduce the count, and do not
split one root cause into several findings.

+ Finding ID:            <F-01>
  Requirement:           <clause or Annex A control, plus the internal policy
                          reference the finding is measured against>
  Evidence seen:         <what was shown, identifier, date, who showed it>
  Condition:             <what was found, stated as fact>
  Effect:                <the consequence if not corrected, in business terms>
  Classification:         <major nonconformity / minor nonconformity /
                          observation / improvement opportunity>
  Root cause:            <the cause agreed with the owner, not the symptom>
  Correction:            <the immediate action taken or planned>
  Corrective action:     <what prevents a repeat>
  Owner:                 <name, role>
  Due date:              <date>
  Re-test evidence:      <what will be checked, and when>
  Status:                <open / in progress / awaiting verification / closed>

+ Finding ID:            <F-02>
  ... repeat the block ...

Classification rule to state once, in the report, so it is not argued later:
  Major      a requirement is not implemented, or a failure reaches a
             regulatory, contractual or material business impact
  Minor      an isolated lapse in an implemented control
  Observation  no requirement breached; a weakness with a plausible future
             impact
  Improvement  no breach and no weakness; a possible gain


6. REQUIREMENTS TESTED WITHOUT FINDINGS
---------------------------------------
List what was tested and found conforming, so the audit file shows coverage:
  <clause or control>  <evidence sampled>  <items tested>
A report that lists only findings cannot prove coverage of clauses 4 to 10.


7. ACTIONS AND FOLLOW-UP
------------------------
Action log:              <finding ID, action, owner, due date, status>
Management review input: <date the results go to the clause 9.3 review>
Re-test plan:            <which items are re-tested, in which cycle>
Escalation:              <what happens when an action passes its due date>


8. LIMITATIONS AND DISCLAIMERS
------------------------------
State plainly what the audit does not cover, for example: this is an internal
audit under clause 9.2, not a certification audit, not a penetration test and
not a legal opinion; sample-based testing gives reasonable assurance, not
proof of completeness; findings reflect the evidence available at the audit
dates.
