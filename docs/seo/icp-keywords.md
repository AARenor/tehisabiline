**Only two offers are in this property's scope:** (a) agent-driven cyber audits and
pentesting under written permission, with human review of every significant finding;
(b) an application-based pilot for the cyber-tuned model API. Managed AI services,
assistants, knowledge bases, automation and startup packages moved to
[arletop.com](https://arletop.com/) on 2026-10-24. Do not target them here.

The existing-system API testing guide (`/api-turvatestimine/`) is not the planned model
API (`/mudelid/`). Authorized security testing of a customer's own AI feature is within
the cyber-testing context; it is not a promise of AI consulting, chatbot development,
knowledge-base setup or generic model evaluation.

Compliance advisers, lawyers/accountants and journalists may use or cite regulatory
guides, but `PRODUCT.md` does not name them as ICPs. Keep them as adjacent readers or
referrers, not owner-confirmed audiences. Training and certification are not offered.

## Research, source labels and dated evidence

Four public-query groups were researched on 2026-10-30. Estonian queries covered NIS2/KüTS
and cyber audit/pentest; English queries covered DORA and CRA/SBOM. Search results were
used to identify intent and source quality, not rank:

- “NIS2 KüTS Eesti ettevõtte kohaldamisala registreerimine RIA ametlik juhend”: [RIA business advice](https://www.ria.ee/kuberturvalisus/kuberruumi-analuus-ja-ennetus/nouanded-asutustele-ja-ettevotetele), [Riigi Teataja KüTS](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/106102026002), and [NIS2 Directive 2022/2555](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj/eng). Prefer the current Estonian law/RIA over third-party summaries.
- “Eesti küberaudit turvaaudit pentest ettevõttele RIA infoturve juhend”: RIA guidance plus first-party service pages. The `turvaaudit` SERP is broader than cyber; label the service as IT/cybersecurity and separate audit from pentest.
- “DORA register of information template TLPT official EBA European Commission”: [EBA DORA preparation](https://www.eba.europa.eu/activities/direct-supervision-and-oversight/digital-operational-resilience-act/preparation-dora-application); use it to distinguish ITS 2024/2956 from a working CSV.
- “Cyber Resilience Act SBOM vulnerability reporting official European Commission regulation 2024/2847”: [European Commission CRA reporting](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting), [Commission CRA summary](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-summary), [Regulation (EU) 2024/2847](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng), and [CISA SBOM guidance](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/information-communications-technology-supply-chain-security/sbom). The Commission page gives the CRA reporting start date and reporting sequence; the Regulation controls over the Commission's expressly non-comprehensive summary. CISA informs SBOM terminology, not EU law.
