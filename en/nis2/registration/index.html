NIS2 registration in Estonia: RIA steps and deadlines

If NIS2 applies, registration is the first visible obligation. This guide covers who must notify RIA, the three-month deadline from becoming a service provider, the two-week change notice, what to prepare and the mistakes that create avoidable follow-up.

By Arle Aron Reidma, Tehisabiline OÜ · Last materially updated 27 October 2026

Short answer

Notify RIA within three months of becoming a service provider within the scope of the Cybersecurity Act (KüTS § 3). Changes in the data you submitted must be notified no later than two weeks after the change. Entities already in scope on 1 January 2026: within three months of the amendment entering into force on 1 January 2026 (that window closed in spring 2026); if you have not notified RIA yet, do it now. Registration is not a compliance certificate; it opens the supervisory relationship and creates a record of the entity, services and contact. For the duties themselves, see NIS2 audit evidence; for scope, see NIS2 in Estonia.

Who must register

Sector alone is not enough, and size alone is not enough. Check whether your activity is listed in KüTS § 3 and whether that category's size test is met. Usually this means at least 50 employees and annual turnover or balance sheet above €10 million. Some categories apply regardless of size. Essential entities in the § 3(3) categories need at least 250 employees and a balance sheet above €43 million or turnover above €50 million. Group data are not added up automatically: under § 3(7), partner and linked-enterprise data are not counted where the entity is independent of them in the systems used to provide the services.

  • Essential and important entities providing services in Estonia.
  • Entities that become service providers after a change in activity or size.
  • Entities whose register data changes.

If you are unsure, document the assessment and ask RIA before assuming you are outside scope.

Registration steps

Notifications are made in the state portal eesti.ee, business view: Cybersecurity → Service provider notification. RIA's guide says a member of the service provider's management board can submit it.

  1. Confirm the obligation: sector, subsector, entity type, services and any group relationships that affect scope.
  2. Prepare the data: name and registry code, registered office or operating address, current contact details (email addresses and phone numbers) and IP address ranges.
  3. Check the current form: do not use an old PDF or a third-party template.
  4. Submit and keep proof: keep the confirmation and a dated copy of the data.
  5. Update: notify changes no later than two weeks after the change.

Example (27 October 2026; not a legal determination): a company becomes a service provider within KüTS § 3 on 15 September 2026. The three-month deadline is 15 December 2026. If its contact person changes on 1 November 2026, the change must be notified no later than 15 November 2026 (two weeks).

Common errors

  • Registering late because the obligation was not assessed.
  • Counting from the start of the business instead of from becoming a service provider.
  • Listing the parent group instead of the legal entity that provides the service.
  • Leaving the security contact generic or outdated.
  • Forgetting the two-week change notice.
  • Treating registration as the end of the project.

Frequently asked questions

When must a NIS2 entity register in Estonia?

Notify RIA within three months of becoming a service provider within KüTS § 3. Changes in the data you submitted must be notified no later than two weeks after the change. Entities already in scope on 1 January 2026: within three months of the amendment entering into force on 1 January 2026 (that window closed in spring 2026); if you have not notified RIA yet, do it now.

What is the NIS2 registration deadline?

The three-month deadline runs from the date you became a service provider within KüTS § 3, not from the start of the business. The two-week deadline runs from the date of the change. The calculator shows calendar dates, not RIA processing time.

Does registration prove NIS2 compliance?

No. Registration opens the supervisory dialogue. It does not replace risk management, the ten duties, incident reporting or evidence.

What should be prepared before registering?

Name and registry code, registered office and operating address, current contact details (email addresses, phone numbers), IP address ranges, sector and subsector where relevant, and the services in scope. Check the current eesti.ee form before submitting.
